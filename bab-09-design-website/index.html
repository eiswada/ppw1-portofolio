Hey, I'm Raya — a Technical Artist based in Indonesia. I specialize in real-time rendering, shader authoring in HLSL/GLSL, and building VFX pipelines that bridge the gap between the art team and the engine. I'm most at home inside Unreal Engine and Unity, scripting tools in Python or Blueprints so artists can iterate fast without waiting on engineering. I care deeply about craft: the kind of care that makes a particle effect feel alive or a material respond exactly the way light should.
